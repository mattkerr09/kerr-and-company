Local SEO FAQs

How much does Google Business Profile management cost?

Managed service runs $500–$1,200/month depending on review automation and number of locations. That covers profile optimization, weekly posts, review generation and responses, citation cleanup, and monthly rank reporting.

How do I show up in the map pack?

It's driven mostly by your Google Business Profile: the right primary category, complete accurate info, consistent name/address/phone across the web, steady review volume and responses, photos, and regular posts — reinforced by a local page on your site.

Can you automate getting more reviews?

Yes — we build a system that texts or emails customers after a job, routes happy customers to your Google link, and sends unhappy ones to a private inbox first so you can fix it. Higher-rated businesses get meaningfully more calls.

Does my business qualify for a profile?

You qualify if you have in-person customer contact — a storefront, or a service-area business that travels to customers. Fully online businesses generally don't, and I'll tell you honestly if local SEO isn't the right spend for you.
