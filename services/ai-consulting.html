AI consulting FAQs

What does an AI consultant actually do?

Tells you where AI will genuinely save money or win customers in your business, which tools to use, what to build versus buy, how to keep your data safe, and how to get your team using it. The deliverable is a decision you can act on — not a slide deck.

How much does AI consulting cost?

Hourly advisory is $250/hour. Most clients prefer fixed scope: an AI Opportunity Assessment at $3,500 (2 weeks), or an advisory retainer from $1,500/month.

Is my business too small for AI?

No — small businesses often see the fastest returns, because one automation (texting back missed calls, requesting reviews) moves real revenue. I'll also tell you honestly when AI isn't the answer and something simpler would work better.

What makes you different from other AI consultants?

We ship our own AI products — a macOS app with a paying customer and 3 provisional patents, a live B2B SaaS, and a 50-agent automation engine. The advice comes from building and supporting real production software, not from reading about it.
