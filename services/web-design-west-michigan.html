What changes town to town, and what doesn't

The build does not change. Every site gets the same mobile-first structure, the same tested forms and click-to-call, the same on-page SEO and speed work, whether the business is on Monroe Center or on US-31.

The local setup does

Local rankings lean on three things you control: the Business Profile, the words on the page, and how consistently the business's name, address and phone appear across the web. For a Holland business we write Holland on the page and in the schema, set the Business Profile's service area to Ottawa County, and fix the listings so they agree. That is the difference between page one in your town and page ten in Grand Rapids.

Seasonal demand does

Lakeshore and resort businesses do most of a year's selling in a few months. The site has to capture the July visitor who books immediately and the February planner who wants a quote emailed. We build both paths, and the care plan keeps the site current between seasons without you thinking about it.

The person you talk to doesn't

You work with the builder from the first call to launch. That is true in Grand Rapids and it is true in Muskegon.
