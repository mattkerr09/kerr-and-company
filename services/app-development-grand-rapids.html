What "one custom AI tool" means, and what it does not

The Growth package is described on the pricing cards as everything in the Business Site plus one custom AI tool — quote, booking or bot — with a priority build and strategy. Each of those words is doing work.

One tool, one job

The tool is scoped to a single job your business needs done: produce a quote range, take a booking, or collect what a first call collects. It is connected to the systems that job needs. The price moves with how many of your existing systems it has to talk to, and one integration is not four; that is why the quote is fixed only after we know which systems are involved.

Custom, not a template with your logo

It is built around how your business actually runs, which is why it keeps working. It is not a rebranded chatbot template, and the demonstrations show the difference: the roofing estimator has your kind of inputs, not a generic "how can I help you" box.

Not a native app

We do not build iOS or Android apps for the App Store or Google Play. The tools are web apps: they live at your web address, open in the browser on any phone, need no install and no store review, and you own the code. For a local business that means a customer taps a link and is using the tool, instead of being asked to download something first.

Not several tools for the price of one

A second tool, or a connected chain of them — intake to quote to follow-up to reporting — is the Automation System, from $7,500, on the AI automation page. A customer portal or anything with logins is scoped on its own. We would rather say that here than discover it in the middle of a build.

Web app or native app: which one you actually need

Native apps exist for good reasons. They can use the phone's hardware directly, they can work with no signal, and they sit on the home screen as an icon. They also have to be built for two platforms, submitted to two stores, updated through two review processes, and installed by every customer before any of it matters.

For most of the local businesses we build for, none of the native advantages applies and every one of the costs does. A homeowner comparing roofers is not going to install an app to get a price range; they will use the one that gives it to them on the web page they are already on. A salon customer books in the browser and moves on. The web tool wins because the customer's part is one tap. If your case is the exception — field crews working offline, a product that genuinely needs the camera or the sensors — we will say so on the first call, tell you what that build looks like, and not pretend a web tool covers it.

If you are building a product rather than a tool for an existing business, that is a different conversation and a different page: studio engagements cover a Build Audit from $3,500, a 0→1 product sprint from $25,000 and an agent build from $15,000. And if the question is whether to build at all, AI consulting answers build-versus-buy for $250 an hour, or as a $3,500 fixed-scope assessment whose plan is yours whether or not we build it.

Questions people ask

Do you build iPhone or Android apps?

No. The tools we build are web-based: they run inside your website, in the browser on any phone, with nothing to install and no app store in the way. If what you need genuinely has to be a native app, we will say so on the first call rather than sell you a web tool that does not fit.

What does the AI part of the tool actually do?

It does the part a person would otherwise do on the first call. An intake bot collects the details you would ask for — address, timing, photos, what is going on — and drops them into your inbox or calendar at any hour. A quote tool turns a few answers into a real range. It is not a rebranded chatbot template; it is built around the job you give it.

How long does a custom tool take?

Most single tools are live in one to two weeks, simple ones in days. A site and tool together get one written date before you commit. What stretches it is the number of your existing systems the tool has to talk to: one integration is not four.

Who owns the tool when it is done?

You do. Code, content, domain and accounts are yours at the end, the same as with every build here. There is no lock-in and no licence; if you leave, you take the tool with you.

Do I have to replace the software I already use?

Usually not. The tool connects to the scheduling, CRM and invoicing software you already run rather than forcing a migration. If your current stack genuinely cannot support what you need, we will tell you before you spend anything.
