SEO FAQs

How much does SEO cost in Grand Rapids?

Here, single-location Local SEO with landing pages is $750 a month, and managing your Google Business Profile alone is $500 a month. Competitive, multi-service businesses run $2,500 a month on Growth SEO. You can also start with a one-time audit and roadmap for $1,500 and keep the plan either way. For comparison, Ahrefs' survey of 439 SEO providers found the most common retainer is $500 to $1,000 a month, and agencies average $3,209 when each is counted at the top of its price band.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile work can move local rankings and calls in 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive searches usually take 4 to 6 months of steady work. Of the eight Grand Rapids agencies on page one in September 2026, the two that state a timeline both name 3 to 6 months. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is selling you something.

Do you require a long contract?

Three months to start, because SEO needs that long to show anything, then month to month. The term is written here before you call, which most local agencies don't do.

Who owns the work if I leave?

You do. The pages, your Google Business Profile, the listings and the analytics accounts are in your name from day one. If you stop, nothing is switched off and nothing has to be handed back.

Can you guarantee page one or the map pack?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Google's own advice on hiring an SEO is to find someone else if they guarantee first place. What I can put in writing is the work, the price and a report that shows whether it is paying.

How many Google reviews do I need?

There is no fixed number. Across the 12 top-three map spots I measured for Grand Rapids trades, review counts ran from 22 to 12,142, with a median of 170. Every one was rated 4.4 or higher, so the rating matters as much as the count.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

For the map pack, yes. Google gives profiles to businesses that meet customers in person, at a shop or by travelling to them. If you qualify and have no profile, that is the first job. If you do not qualify, I will tell you before you pay, and the work goes into your site instead.

What is included in an SEO retainer?

Technical audits and fixes, on-page work, keyword and content planning, new pages, Google Business Profile management, citations, and reporting on rankings, traffic, calls and form leads.

Why is this site not first for "Grand Rapids SEO"?

Fair question. The site went up in July 2026, and the map pack for that search goes to firms with a local address and a Google profile. I work remotely, so this studio has no map listing of its own.
