SEO FAQs

How much does SEO cost in Grand Rapids?

Single-location Local SEO is $750/month. Competitive, multi-service businesses run $2,500/month on Growth SEO. You can also start with a one-time audit and roadmap ($1,500) and keep the plan either way.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile work can move local rankings and calls in 4–8 weeks. Competitive keyword rankings usually take 4–6 months of consistent work. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is selling you something.

What's included in a retainer?

Technical audits and fixes, on-page optimization, keyword and content strategy, content production, Google Business Profile management, citations, and a live dashboard showing rankings, traffic, and calls.

Do you require a long contract?

No long lock-in. SEO needs consistency, so I recommend a 3-month initial commitment, then month to month. You own every page, profile, and asset we build.
