Automation FAQs

What can AI automation actually do for a small business?

The highest-value ones are simple: texting back every missed call, requesting reviews after each job, routing new leads to your phone in seconds, generating instant quotes, and pulling your numbers into one dashboard. These recover revenue you're already losing.

How much does a custom automation cost?

A focused build starts at $2,500; a multi-step system starts at $7,500 and scales with integrations. Hosting, monitoring, and improvements start at $300/month depending on complexity. You get a fixed quote before any work begins.

How long does it take?

Most single automations are live in one to two weeks, simple ones in days. We build with AI ourselves — that's why we ship faster than agencies quoting two-month timelines.

Usually not. We connect to the scheduling, CRM, and invoicing tools you already use instead of forcing a migration. If your stack genuinely can't support what you need, I'll tell you before you spend anything.
