Ads FAQs

How much does ads management cost?

A one-time $500 campaign setup, then flat monthly management from $750 for local accounts under $5,000/mo in ad spend, and $1,500 for multi-platform Growth Ads. Flat fees mean your cost doesn't rise just because you spend more.

What ad budget do I need?

Most local businesses start meaningfully at $10–$50/day ($300–$1,500/month). One campaign generated 47 tour leads on $179 total spend — small budgets work when the offer and targeting are right.

What results have you actually gotten?

Real-estate tour campaigns at $3.82 per lead — about 4x below the ~$17 industry median for real-estate lead campaigns on Meta (WordStream, 2025) — and an e-commerce store driving $11K+ in sales at 4.08% conversion.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. Flat monthly fees keep your cost predictable and keep my incentives honest — I don't get paid more for telling you to spend more. You pay the platforms directly and own your ad accounts and data.
