Results are not guaranteed

Performance figures on this site — including cost per lead, conversion rates, sales, traffic, follower counts, downloads, and rankings — describe specific past campaigns or projects under specific conditions. They are examples, not promises. Past results do not guarantee, predict, or imply future results.

Marketing outcomes depend on many factors outside our control: your market, budget, offer, pricing, competition, industry, seasonality, sales process, response times, and the policies and algorithms of third-party platforms. Two businesses running identical campaigns routinely get different results. Your results will differ.

Specifically, we do not guarantee any particular search ranking, ad placement, impression volume, lead volume, cost per lead, conversion rate, sale, revenue figure, or return on investment.

About the figures we cite

Where we reference our own results — such as a $3.82 cost per real-estate tour lead — those figures come from actual campaigns we ran and reflect the spend, market, offer, and timeframe of those specific campaigns. Where we compare against an industry figure (such as an approximately $17 median cost per lead for real-estate lead campaigns on Meta), that figure is drawn from a published third-party benchmark believed accurate as of the date cited. Benchmarks change over time and vary by methodology, market, and source.

All prices shown on this site are starting points and estimates. They are not offers, and they do not create a binding agreement. Final pricing depends on scope, complexity, integrations, and requirements, and is set only in a written proposal or agreement signed by both parties. Prices may change at any time without notice. Third-party costs — advertising spend, hosting, domains, software licenses, stock media — are separate from our fees.

Third-party pricing, timelines, and service inclusions vary widely and change frequently. Any specific provider may offer terms better or worse than the ranges shown.
