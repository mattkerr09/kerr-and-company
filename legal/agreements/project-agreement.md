## 2. Payment

**50% to start. 50% on launch.** The first payment starts the work; the second
is due when the site goes live.

Larger builds can be split differently — ask before signing, and whatever we
agree gets written into §1 rather than remembered.

## 3. What you get, and what you own

On final payment, **all of it is yours**: the code, the content we wrote, the
domain, and every account we created for the project. No licence, no lock-in,
no ongoing fee to keep using what you paid for.

You can take it to another developer the next day and we will hand over cleanly.

## 4. Revisions

**Three rounds are included.** A round means you send us your collected changes
and we make them.

Anything beyond three rounds is **quoted in writing and agreed before a minute
of work happens.** We will never do extra work and bill you for it afterwards.

## 5. What we do, and what you supply

**We write the copy.** You do not hand us finished text and hope. If you have
existing copy you want kept, tell us before we start.

**You supply photographs and brand assets** — or tell us to use stock images,
which we will. **Logo design is quoted separately** and is not part of this
agreement.

## 6. Not included in the price

- Photography and logo design
- Your domain registration fee, your ad spend, and any paid third-party service
  you ask us to connect
- **Hosting after launch**, unless you take a Care Plan. A static site can be
  handed to you to host free — we will show you how
- **Regulated copy** — medical, legal or financial claims needing a licensed
  specialist to approve. We will tell you before we start if your project needs
  this, not after

## 7. Timeline, and what moves it

We give you a **date in writing before you commit**. We hold to it.

Two things move it, and both are yours: **supplying the photos and brand assets
from §5**, and **answering questions**. If we are waiting on you, the date moves
by the length of the wait. It does not change the price.

Normal turnarounds here are days rather than weeks. If you need it faster than
that, say so before signing — compressing further is possible and costs more.

## 8. If you cancel

You may cancel at any time, in writing.

- **Before we start:** full refund of the deposit.
- **After we start:** you pay for the work done to that point, and we invoice or
  refund the difference against your deposit. You receive everything produced so
  far — files, copy, and anything already built.

We do not keep a deposit for work we have not done.

## 9. If we cancel

We may end this agreement if you ask us to publish content that is unlawful,
that we reasonably believe to be false, or that requires a licence we do not
hold. In that case we refund **every payment made** and nothing transfers.

## 10. After launch

Thirty days of **fixes** are included — anything that does not work as agreed,
we repair at no cost.

That is separate from **changes**, which are new work and are quoted. A Care
Plan at $199/mo covers hosting, backups, monitoring and ongoing edits if you
would rather not think about it.

## 11. Plain terms

- **Michigan law**, and the courts of Kent County, Michigan.
- If any part is unenforceable, the rest stands.
- This is the whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both of
  us. **Anything agreed in conversation and not written here is not part of the
  deal** — which protects you as much as us.
- We may show the finished site as an example of our work. Tell us in writing if
  you would rather we did not, and we will not.
